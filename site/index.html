Publications

  • DBRAG: Multi-Table Retrieval-Augmented Generation for Complex Database Queries

    Prince Larbi Ampofo, Ryoji Kubo, Djellel Difallah

    AACL-IJCNLP 2026 · Accepted, main conference

    DBRAG answers questions whose evidence is spread across multiple database tables. It retrieves and reranks relevant tables, then uses program-aided reasoning over their full contents while keeping the language model’s initial context compact.

  • RAW-Explainer: Post-hoc Explanations of Path Reasoning Graph Neural Networks for Knowledge Graph Completion

    Ryoji Kubo, Samyam Lamichhane, Djellel Difallah

    Learning on Graphs 2026 · Accepted

    RAW-Explainer explains knowledge-graph link predictions by finding small, connected subgraphs through a learned random-walk objective. A separate robust evaluator helps assess these explanations when their distribution differs from the full graph.

  • Composable Crystals: Controllable Materials Discovery via Concept Learning

    Nian Liu, Yuwei Zeng, Ryoji Kubo, Nikita Kazeev, Stephen Gregory Dale, Artem Maevskiy, Pengru Huang, Thomas Laurent, Kostya S. Novoselov, Xavier Bresson

    Preprint · 2026

    We learn reusable crystal concepts that capture local atomic environments and global symmetry, then recombine them to guide the generation of new materials. This gives more control over exploration beyond known structures and improves the discovery of novel crystals.

  • Crys-JEPA: Accelerating Crystal Discovery via Embedding Screening and Generative Refinement

    Nian Liu, Nikita Kazeev, Stephen Gregory Dale, Artem Maevskiy, Yuwei Zeng, Ryoji Kubo, Pengru Huang, Thomas Laurent, Yann LeCun, Kostya S. Novoselov, Xavier Bresson

    Preprint · 2026

    Crys-JEPA learns crystal representations that preserve differences in formation energy, allowing candidate stability to be assessed through embedding comparisons. Promising candidates are then used to refine the generator, improving the search for crystals that are both stable and novel.

  • XGExplainer: Robust Evaluation-based Explanation for Graph Neural Networks

    Ryoji Kubo, Djellel Difallah

    SIAM International Conference on Data Mining (SDM) · 2024

    XGExplainer improves graph-neural-network explanations by training a separate evaluator on randomized subgraphs. This helps existing explanation methods recognize informative subgraphs even when they differ from the data used to train the original prediction model.
